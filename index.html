01关于About

我在做什么What I work on

我的训练来自统计学：数理统计、贝叶斯统计、统计预测与决策、大数据案例分析构成课程主干，高维筛选、惩罚回归、生存分析、因果推断与抽样设计是反复用到的工具。这套工具落在四种很不一样的问题上——一篇超高维特征筛选的学术论文、一次受企业委托的市场调查、一套企业破产预警的可解释模型，以及一次电商漏斗的批判式复现。 My training is in statistics — mathematical statistics, Bayesian inference, forecasting and decision theory, large-scale data case studies — with high-dimensional screening, penalized regression, survival analysis, causal inference and sampling design as the working toolkit. That toolkit lands on four very different problems: an academic paper on ultrahigh-dimensional feature screening, a client-commissioned market survey, an interpretable corporate bankruptcy-warning model, and a critical reproduction of an e-commerce funnel analysis.

工作习惯围绕可核验展开：脚本编号化、随机种子固定、每一步结果落盘，README 里写清楚已知局限。破产预警的 16 个幸存特征每一个都能回溯到筛选与惩罚回归的中间结果；因果推断那篇论文的主要结论是一次不显著的零效应；电商漏斗里，我指出自己最亮眼的指标存在循环论证。这些判断不讨巧，但它们更接近真实的分析工作。 The working habit is built around verifiability: numbered scripts, fixed random seeds, every step written to disk, and known limitations stated in the README. Each of the 16 surviving features in the bankruptcy model traces back to intermediate screening and penalized-regression output; the headline result of the causal-inference paper is an insignificant null; and in the funnel project I flag that my most striking indicator is circular by construction. Those are not the flattering conclusions, but they are closer to real analytical work.

接下来想补的是方法的另一半：SQL 进阶取数、可复现的数据管线，以及把一个模型真正交付出去、而不止停在报告里的能力。 What I want to add next is the other half of the craft: advanced SQL, reproducible data pipelines, and the ability to ship a model rather than stopping at the report.

03 论文Paper 方法学底盘Methods core

GCor-SIS：超高维生存数据的稳健特征筛选GCor-SIS: robust feature screening for ultrahigh-dimensional survival data

无模型筛选 · FDR 控制 · 重尾稳健性 · 生存分析Model-free screening · FDR control · heavy-tail robustness · survival analysis

科研 / 论文Research paper · 第二作者（第一作者：导师姜云卢教授）· 投稿 Communications in Statistics – Simulation and Computation，审稿返修中 Second author (first author: Prof. Yunlu Jiang) · submitted to Communications in Statistics – Simulation and Computation, under revision · 2026-05 于中国人民大学第二届全国高校本科生统计论坛作报告 Presented at the 2nd National Undergraduate Statistics Forum, Renmin University of China (2026-05)

基于 Pearson 相关的筛选方法在重尾分布下矩假设失效。这项工作参与提出基于格罗滕迪克相关的无模型（model-free）筛选方法 GCor-SIS，引入数据拆分反射实现 FDR 控制，并首次把框架推广到生存数据。我负责部分关键引理的证明、R 从零实现算法、重尾场景的蒙特卡洛模拟，以及论文主笔撰写。 Pearson-correlation screeners fail under heavy tails because their moment assumptions break down. This work proposes GCor-SIS, a model-free screener based on Grothendieck's correlation, adds a data-splitting reflection for FDR control, and extends the framework to survival data for the first time. I proved part of the key lemmas, implemented the algorithm in R from scratch, ran the heavy-tailed Monte Carlo study, and wrote the manuscript.

04 回归实证Regression

北京住房价格：特征价格模型与多元回归实证Beijing housing prices: a hedonic model with regression diagnostics

半对数设定 · 双向固定效应 · 六模型对照 · 完整诊断Semi-log form · two-way fixed effects · six-model comparison · full diagnostics

课程论文Course paper · 独立完成 · 现代回归分析 Sole author · Modern Regression Analysis

用带时间—区域双向固定效应的半对数特征价格模型，估计各项住房特征的隐含价格。核心不在于把 R² 推高，而在于量化两个会被朴素统计系统性误读的效应：近地铁溢价被朴素对比高估约 4.8 倍，而挂牌天数缺失本身也携带信息。 A semi-log hedonic model with two-way (time × district) fixed effects estimates the implicit price of each housing attribute. The point is not a higher R² but quantifying two effects that naive statistics systematically misread: the subway-proximity premium is overstated roughly 4.8× by a naive comparison, and missing listing-days carries information in itself.

05 因果推断Causal

AI 采纳与生产率：双重机器学习因果推断AI adoption and productivity: a double/debiased machine learning study

Neyman 正交评分 · 交叉拟合 · 因果森林异质性 · 安慰剂检验Neyman-orthogonal score · cross-fitting · causal-forest heterogeneity · placebo tests

自主研究Independent research · 独立完成 · 工作论文（含完整 LaTeX 论文与可复现代码） Sole author · working paper with a full LaTeX manuscript and reproducible code

在 15 万家企业的微观数据上，用双重机器学习在控制 64 维企业特征后估计 AI 采纳对劳动生产率的平均因果效应，并完成共同支撑与安慰剂检验。核心结果是一次不显著的零效应：把它写成可辩护的结论，和写成一个显著结果同样需要证据。 Using double/debiased machine learning on 150,000 firms, I estimated the causal effect of AI adoption on labour productivity after controlling for 64 firm characteristics, with overlap and placebo diagnostics. The headline result is an insignificant null — making “no effect” defensible takes the same evidence as making an effect significant.
