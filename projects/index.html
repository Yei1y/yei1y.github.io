评分口径Scoring rubric · 本人自评（1–5 分），非外部认证。含金量 = 稀缺性 × 难度 × 背书（5 分：SCI 投稿 / 国家级奖项 / 方法创新 + 导师背书；4 分：省级一等奖 / 自主提出研究问题；3 分：省级三等奖 / 优秀课程作业；2 分：练习复现类，无外部背书）。完成度 = 可交付 × 可复现 × 闭环（5 分：论文 + 代码 + 结果全落盘，数字可逐项核验）。岗位匹配度 = 对数据科学 / 数据分析岗的直接对口程度（5 分：SQL + 业务指标 + 归因 + 实验）。加权 = 含金量 × 0.4 + 完成度 × 0.3 + 匹配度 × 0.3。 All scores are my own self-assessment (1–5), not external accreditation. Substance = scarcity × difficulty × endorsement (5: SCI submission / national award / methodological novelty with supervisor backing; 4: provincial 1st prize / self-posed research question; 3: provincial 3rd prize / strong coursework; 2: reproduction with no external endorsement). Completeness = deliverable × reproducibility × closure (5: paper + code + every result persisted and checkable). Role fit = directness of match to a data science / analytics role (5: SQL + business metrics + attribution + experimentation). Weighted = substance × 0.4 + completeness × 0.3 + role fit × 0.3.

含金量最高的是第 3 名The highest-substance project ranks third

GCor-SIS 的含金量是全项目最高的 5.0，但它是纯科研：没有业务语境，也没有指标体系，对数据分析岗的匹配度只有 3.5。企业破产预警排在前面，是因为它同时踩住三条线——学术上有省级奖项背书，工程上有 20 张可核验的结果表与 Rcpp 并行优化，业务上有代价敏感决策与利润测算。 GCor-SIS scores the highest substance (5.0), but it is pure research: no business context, no metric system, and only 3.5 on role fit. The bankruptcy model ranks first because it hits all three lines at once — a provincial award for substance, 20 checkable result tables plus Rcpp parallelism for engineering, and cost-sensitive decisions with profit projections for business.

第 2 名与第 1 名相差 0.01 分No. 2 trails No. 1 by 0.01

市场调查有三个别的项目没有的东西：企业委托（不是自选题目）、一手数据（841 + 476 分钟访谈、747 + 444 份问卷、4,000 条评论，全部自采）、以及完整闭环（抽样设计 → 数据分析 → 人群细分 → SEM 决策路径 → 分市场策略 → 路演答辩 → 大创结项）。这条链路上每一环都有产出，它也是最适合讲成完整案例的一个。 The market survey has three things the others lack: a client commission (not a self-chosen topic), first-hand data (841 + 476 minutes of interviews, 747 + 444 questionnaires, 4,000 reviews — all self-collected), and a complete loop (sampling design → analysis → segmentation → SEM decision path → market-specific strategy → pitch → project completion). Every link in that chain produced an artefact, which makes it the easiest project to tell as a full case study.

第 6 名的岗位匹配度是唯一满分：SQL 取数、指标体系、漏斗归因、转化建模，正是数据分析岗的日常。它排位靠后，原因是公开项目复现、完成度还在 3.0——这是目前性价比最高的一块补强，补齐后可直接进入前四。 No. 6 holds the only perfect role-fit score: SQL querying, metric definitions, funnel attribution and conversion modelling are exactly an analyst's day job. It sits low because it reproduces a public project and its completeness is still 3.0 — the cheapest gap to close right now, and closing it moves the project into the top four.

GCor-SIS：超高维生存数据的稳健特征筛选GCor-SIS: robust screening for ultrahigh-dimensional survival data

工作论文 · 独立完成（含 LaTeX 论文与代码）Working paper · sole author (LaTeX + code)

08课程论文Course paper

THUCNews 中文新闻分类：LSTM vs 朴素贝叶斯THUCNews classification: LSTM vs naive Bayes

自然语言处理 · 独立完成NLP · sole author

20 万条新闻、10 个类别上的深度模型与传统基线对照。真正的发现不是 LSTM 更准，而是基线的分词修正后差距从 59 个百分点缩到 2.1 个百分点——基线没调好时的对比会严重高估深度学习带来的收益。 A deep model against a classical baseline on 200,000 news articles across 10 classes. The real finding is not that LSTM wins, but that fixing the baseline's tokenisation shrank the gap from 59 to 2.1 percentage points — an untuned baseline badly overstates the benefit of deep learning.

  • LSTM 测试 acc 0.8868；朴素贝叶斯 0.2967 → 分词修正后 0.8658LSTM test acc 0.8868; naive Bayes 0.2967 → 0.8658 after fixing tokenisation
  • 差距从 59 pp 修正为 2.10 ppGap corrected from 59 pp to 2.10 pp
