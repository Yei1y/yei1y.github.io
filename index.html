About

Who I Am

A statistics student passionate about understanding the world through data.

I'm an undergraduate student in Economic Statistics, about to begin my Master's journey in Applied Statistics. My academic interests center on causal inference, statistical modeling, and machine learning — with a strong emphasis on methodological rigor and reproducible research.

I believe that good statistics is good storytelling: every dataset has a narrative waiting to be uncovered, and the role of a statistician is to let that story emerge honestly and clearly.

When I'm not running regressions or debugging Python scripts, you'll find me reading about Bayesian methods, tinkering with NLP models, or exploring Beijing's café scene.
